## 2. What we collect and why

We collect only what each feature needs. Every purpose below is shown to you in plain language, in English or a language of the Eighth Schedule (we offer Hindi), before we ask for consent.

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Name, phone, email | Account, OTP sign-in, booking confirmations | Consent |

| KYC details (clients and lawyers) | Verifying identity; for lawyers, verifying Bar Council enrolment | Consent; legal obligation where applicable |

| Ratings and reviews | Showing other clients verified feedback | Consent |

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

### Sensitive information

Legal problems often involve highly personal matters (health, family, criminal allegations, finances). Please share only what you need to. Messages with your lawyer are confidential between you and them; our staff do not read them except to investigate an abuse report you or the lawyer file, or where the law requires it.

## 3. Children

LawyerUp is for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, a parent or lawful guardian must create the account and give verifiable consent (DPDP Act, s. 9). We do not track, profile, or target advertising at children.

## 4. Your rights as a Data Principal

You can, at any time, from **Account → Privacy** or by writing to the Grievance Officer:

- **Access** a summary of your data and who we shared it with (s. 11)
- **Correct, complete, update or erase** your data (s. 12)
- **Withdraw consent** as easily as you gave it (s. 6(4)). Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (s. 14)
- **Grievance redressal** with us, then the Data Protection Board of India (s. 13)

## 5. The AI assistant

"Know Your Rights" is powered by a third-party large language model: [[Anthropic's Claude / Google's Gemini — keep only the one you use in production]]. When you ask a question:

- Your question is sent to that provider to generate the answer. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms and Google's **paid** Gemini API terms, the provider acts as our processor and does not train its models on API inputs.
- *Drafting note, delete before publishing:* Google's **free** Gemini tier allows Google to use prompts to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. Use the free tier only for development and demos, never with real users' legal questions.
- We strip obvious identifiers (phone numbers, email addresses, Aadhaar and PAN numbers) before sending.
- We keep conversations for [[30]] days for abuse prevention and quality review, then delete them, unless you delete them sooner.
- The assistant gives **general legal information, not legal advice**. See the AI Disclaimer.

## 6. Who we share data with

- **The lawyer you book**, only the details needed for the consultation.
- **Processors** acting on our instructions: cloud hosting [[provider, region]], SMS/OTP [[provider]], payments [[provider]], AI model provider ([[Anthropic or Google]]).
- **Authorities**, only when Indian law requires it.

Data may be processed outside India where the DPDP Act permits it and the country is not restricted by the Central Government.

## 7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as the purpose needs it, then erase it. Accounts inactive for [[3 years]] are erased after 48 hours' notice. Booking and payment records are kept for the period tax and accounting laws require.

## 8. Security and breaches

We use encryption in transit, access controls and logging. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Rules require.

## 9. Changes

We will notify you in the app before material changes take effect.
