## 1. What LawyerUp is (and is not)

LawyerUp is a **technology platform and directory**. It helps you (a) read general legal information and (b) find and book consultations with independent advocates enrolled with a State Bar Council.

- **LawyerUp is not a law firm and does not practise law.** No advocate–client relationship is formed with LawyerUp.
- An advocate–client relationship forms only between you and the advocate you choose, on their own terms.
- Advocates listed on LawyerUp are independent. They are not our employees or agents.

## 2. Bar Council compliance

Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules prohibits advocates from soliciting work or advertising. To respect it:

- Listings show only factual information advocates are permitted to publish (name, enrolment number and State Bar Council, qualifications, years of practice, areas of practice, languages, office location, and consultation fee).
- We do **not** rank advocates by payment, sell "featured" placements, or let advocates write promotional claims or testimonials about themselves.
- Search results are ordered by objective criteria you choose (distance, fee, availability, language, verified client rating). The default ordering is explained on the search screen.
- **No fee-sharing.** LawyerUp does not take a percentage of an advocate's professional fee (BCI Rules, Part VI, Ch. II, Sec. IV). Advocates pay a flat platform subscription; clients pay the advocate's fee as shown, plus any separately disclosed platform convenience fee.

## 3. Your account

You must be 18 or over, or use LawyerUp through a parent or guardian. Keep your login secure. Give accurate information, especially during KYC.

## 4. Advocate obligations

Advocates must hold a valid enrolment and certificate of practice, keep listing information accurate, follow the BCI Rules on professional conduct, and keep client communications confidential. We may suspend a listing that cannot be verified.

## 5. Fees, cancellations and refunds

Each advocate's fee is shown before you book, including taxes. [[Cancellation window and refund policy]]. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within [[7]] working days.

## 6. Reviews

Only clients whose case was closed through LawyerUp may review that advocate, once per case. Reviews must be honest and must not disclose confidential case details. We remove reviews that are abusive, fake or defamatory, and we do not let advocates pay to remove or hide reviews.

## 7. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the platform: no false identities, harassment, attempts to circumvent bookings to avoid fees, scraping, or uploading unlawful content. Do not use the AI assistant to plan anything unlawful.

## 8. AI assistant

See the AI Disclaimer. AI answers are general information, can be wrong, and are not a substitute for an advocate's advice.

## 9. Liability

To the extent the law allows, LawyerUp is not liable for the professional advice or conduct of any advocate, or for decisions you make based on AI-generated information. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under Indian law.
