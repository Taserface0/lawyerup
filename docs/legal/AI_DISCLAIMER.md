## Short version (shown above the chat)

**English:** This is an AI assistant. It gives general legal information about Indian law, not legal advice, and it can make mistakes. Check important points with a verified lawyer. In an emergency, call 112.

**Hinglish:** Yeh ek AI assistant hai. Yeh Indian law ki general jaankari deta hai, legal advice nahi, aur isse galti ho sakti hai. Zaroori baatein kisi verified lawyer se confirm karein. Emergency mein 112 call karein.

## Full version

1. **Information, not advice.** Answers explain what the law generally says. Your situation may differ in ways that change the answer.
2. **Can be wrong or out of date.** Laws change. For example, from 1 July 2024 the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) replaced the IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act for new matters. The assistant cites the sections it relies on so you can check them.
3. **No advocate–client relationship.** Talking to the assistant does not make LawyerUp, Anthropic or any advocate your lawyer, and is not privileged.
4. **Emergencies.** If you or someone else is in danger, call **112** (emergency), **1091 / 181** (women helpline), **1098** (child helpline) or **1930** (cyber fraud). For free legal aid, contact your District Legal Services Authority or call NALSA on **15100**.
5. **Privacy.** Don't type Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card numbers. See the Privacy Notice for how AI conversations are handled.
6. **Deadlines matter.** Limitation periods and bail timelines can be short. If a deadline might apply, speak to an advocate now.
